Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (5 Marks)
Q1.
An unbiased coin is tossed once, the probability of getting head is
(a) 8/4
(b) 1
(c) 1/2
(d) 5/7
Answer: (c) 1/2

Probability of getting head when tossing an unbiased coin = 1/2 (or 50%)

Q2.
Which statement is true about proportions?
(a) 2:3 = 4:5
(b) 2:3 = 4:6
(c) 2:3 ≠ 4:5
(d) 2:3 ≠ 4:6
Answer: (b) 2:3 = 4:6

2:3 = 4:6 ⟹ 2 × 6 = 3 × 4 ⟹ 12 = 12 ✓

Q3.
If l || m and m || n, what is the relationship between l and n?
(a) l || n
(b) l ⊥ n
(c) l intersects n
(d) l # n
Answer: (a) l || n

If l || m and m || n, then l || n (Transitive property of parallel lines)

Q4.
Assertion (A): When two parallel lines are intersected by transversal, the angles of any pair of alternate angles are supplementary.
Reason (R): When two parallel lines are intersected by transversal, the angles of any pair of corresponding angles are equal.
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true but R is false
(d) A is false but R is true
Answer: (d) A is false but R is true

Alternate angles are equal (not supplementary) when parallel lines are cut by transversal. Corresponding angles are equal (R is true).

Q5.
There are 10 marbles in a box marked with distinct numbers from 1 to 10. A marble is drawn randomly. Find the probability of getting a prime numbered marble.
Answer: 4/10 = 2/5

Prime numbers from 1 to 10: 2, 3, 5, 7 (4 prime numbers)

Probability = 4/10 = 2/5 = 0.4 or 40%

Section B: Short Answer Questions (10 Marks)
Q6.
Express the ratio 6:7 in its simplest form.
Answer: 6:7

The ratio 6:7 is already in its simplest form because GCD(6,7) = 1

Q7.
If 8:x :: 16:35, then find the value of x.
Answer: x = 17.5

8:x :: 16:35

8 × 35 = 16 × x

280 = 16x

x = 280/16 = 17.5

Q8.
The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2:3:7. Find the largest and smallest angle.
Answer: Smallest = 30°, Largest = 105°

Angles are in ratio 2:3:7

Total parts = 2 + 3 + 7 = 12

Sum of angles in triangle = 180°

Each part = 180/12 = 15°

Smallest angle = 2 × 15° = 30°

Largest angle = 7 × 15° = 105°

Q9.
One of the angles of a triangle is 70° and the other two angles are equal. Find these two angles.
Answer: 55°, 55°

One angle = 70°

Other two equal angles = x each

70 + x + x = 180°

70 + 2x = 180°

2x = 110°

x = 55°

Q10.
The angles of a triangle are (x + 21)°, x° and (2x - 45)°. Find the value of x.
Answer: x = 51°

(x + 21) + x + (2x - 45) = 180°

4x - 24 = 180°

4x = 204°

x = 51°

Section C: Medium Answer Questions (10 Marks)
Q11.
Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If 8 is added to each number, the ratio becomes 2:3. Find the numbers.
Answer: 24 and 40

Let the numbers be 3k and 5k

(3k + 8):(5k + 8) = 2:3

3(3k + 8) = 2(5k + 8)

9k + 24 = 10k + 16

k = 8

Numbers: 3(8) = 24 and 5(8) = 40

Verification: (24+8):(40+8) = 32:48 = 2:3 ✓

Q12.
Three numbers are in the ratio 2:3:5 and the sum of these numbers is 800. Find the numbers.
Answer: 160, 240, 400

Let numbers be 2k, 3k, 5k

2k + 3k + 5k = 800

10k = 800

k = 80

Numbers: 2(80) = 160, 3(80) = 240, 5(80) = 400

Q13.
The ages of two persons are in the ratio 5:7. Eighteen years ago their ages were in the ratio 8:13. Find their present ages.
Answer: Present ages are 50 and 70 years

Let ages be 5k and 7k

18 years ago: (5k - 18):(7k - 18) = 8:13

13(5k - 18) = 8(7k - 18)

65k - 234 = 56k - 144

9k = 90

k = 10

Present ages: 5(10) = 50 and 7(10) = 70 years

Verification: (50-18):(70-18) = 32:52 = 8:13 ✓

Q14.
The boys and the girls in a school are in the ratio 7:4. If total strength of the school be 550, find the number of boys and girls.
Answer: Boys = 350, Girls = 200

Ratio 7:4, Total = 550

Boys = (7/11) × 550 = 350

Girls = (4/11) × 550 = 200

Q15.
The ratio of monthly income to the savings of a family is 7:2. If the savings be of Rs. 500, find the income and expenditure.
Answer: Income = ₹1750, Expenditure = ₹1250

Ratio 7:2, Savings = ₹500

2 parts = ₹500, so 1 part = ₹250

Income = 7 × 250 = ₹1750

Expenditure = 1750 - 500 = ₹1250

Section D: Problem Solving (20 Marks)
Q16.
A man goes 10 m due east and then 24 m due north. Find the distance from the starting point.
Answer: 26 m

Using Pythagoras theorem:

Distance = √(10² + 24²) = √(100 + 576) = √676 = 26 m

Q17.
Two poles of heights 6 m and 11 m stand on a plane ground. If the distance between their feet is 12 m, find the distance between their tops.
Answer: 13 m

Height difference = 11 - 6 = 5 m

Horizontal distance = 12 m

Distance between tops = √(5² + 12²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13 m

Q18.
A chimney 20 m high casts a shadow of length 25 m at 11 am. At the same time, a power line casts a shadow of length 40 m. Find the height of the power line.
Answer: 32 m

Height of chimney/Length of shadow = Height of line/Length of shadow

20/25 = h/40

h = (20 × 40)/25 = 800/25 = 32 m

Q19.
In a cricket match, a batsman hits a boundary 6 times out of 30 balls he plays. Find the probability that he (i) hit a boundary (ii) did not hit a boundary
Answer:

(i) Probability of hitting a boundary = 6/30 = 1/5 = 0.2 or 20%

(ii) Probability of not hitting a boundary = 24/30 = 4/5 = 0.8 or 80%

Q20.
A dice is thrown 100 times and the outcomes are noted. When a dice is thrown at random, find the probability of getting: (i) 2 (ii) 6
Outcome 1 2 3 4 5 6
Frequency 11 15 21 19 20 14
Answer:

(i) Probability of getting 2 = 15/100 = 3/20 = 0.15 or 15%

(ii) Probability of getting 6 = 14/100 = 7/50 = 0.14 or 14%

Q21.
Of the three angles of a triangle, one is three times the smallest and the other is five times the smallest. Find the angles.
Answer: 20°, 60°, 100°

Let smallest angle = x

Angles: x, 3x, 5x

x + 3x + 5x = 180°

9x = 180°

x = 20°

Angles: 20°, 60°, 100°

Q22.
One of the exterior angles of a triangle is 80° and the interior opposite angles are in the ratio 3:5. Find the angles of the triangle.
Answer: 30°, 50°, 100°

Exterior angle = 80° (equal to sum of opposite interior angles)

Let opposite angles be 3k and 5k

3k + 5k = 80°

8k = 80°

k = 10°

Opposite angles: 30° and 50°

Third angle: 180° - 80° = 100°

Q23.
Shivkumar sold one of his properties for ₹72,00,000. He divided this money between his sons Atul and Praful in the ratio 7:11. Find the amount Praful received.
Answer: ₹44,00,000

Ratio 7:11, Total = ₹72,00,000

Praful's share = (11/18) × 72,00,000 = ₹44,00,000

Atul's share = (7/18) × 72,00,000 = ₹28,00,000

Section E: Geometry & Advanced Problems (15 Marks)
Q24.
Find the fourth proportional to 3, 5 and 6.
Answer: 10

Let fourth proportional be x

3:5 :: 6:x

3x = 5 × 6

3x = 30

x = 10

Q25.
Find the fourth proportional to 11, 15 and 22.
Answer: 30

Let fourth proportional be x

11:15 :: 22:x

11x = 15 × 22

11x = 330

x = 30

Q26.
Find the third proportional to 2, 9 and 15.
Answer: 67.5

Using the sequence: 2, 9, 15, x

2:9 :: 15:x

2x = 9 × 15

2x = 135

x = 67.5

Q27.
Find the third proportional to 11, 13 and 26.
Answer: 15.36 (approximately)

Let third proportional be x

11:13 :: 13:x (assuming standard form)

11x = 13 × 13

11x = 169

x = 169/11 ≈ 15.36

Q28.
Find the mean proportional between (i) 6 and 24 (ii) 3 and 27 (iii) 0.4 and 0.9
Answer:

(i) Mean proportional between 6 and 24 = √(6 × 24) = √144 = 12

(ii) Mean proportional between 3 and 27 = √(3 × 27) = √81 = 9

(iii) Mean proportional between 0.4 and 0.9 = √(0.4 × 0.9) = √0.36 = 0.6

Section F: Additional Problems
Q29.
The sides of a triangle are in the ratio 1:2:3. If the perimeter is 36 cm, find its sides.
Answer: 6 cm, 12 cm, 18 cm

Let sides be k, 2k, 3k

k + 2k + 3k = 36

6k = 36

k = 6

Sides: 6 cm, 12 cm, 18 cm
